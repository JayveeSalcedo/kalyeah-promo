Agawan Base

How a round goes

The base-stealing game every Filipino kid played after school, now on one street seen from above. Everyone starts equal: 100 HP, the same speed, the same hitbox.

  1. Throw what's in your hand

    Four cards sit on your tray, the fifth waits as Next. Tap a card to throw it, drag to aim. Each one costs ice water, which fills while you play.

  2. Tag them

    Bring a kid to 0 HP and they're tagged: off to the sidewalk beside their rescue area, out of the round until a friend comes.

  3. Save your friends

    Stand in your rescue area for 5 s to free the teammate who got tagged first. Every rescue restarts a 10 s countdown, so pick your moment.

  4. Take their post

    Hold the enemy base for 12 s alone, faster with friends, or tag their whole team. After 3:00 the round goes to overtime and a capture takes only 3 s. First to 3 wins the match.

__KIDS__
Fair play

Kids are cosmetic only. Same HP, same speed, same hitbox. The only thing that wins is how you play your hand.

14 cards, one deck

Weapons from the games you grew up with

Nobody picks weapons. Every card is in one shuffled deck that cycles: throw one and it goes to the back, the next one slides in. The number on each card is its ice water cost.
