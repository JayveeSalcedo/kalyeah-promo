Three street games

Pick your game

The games every kid on the street knows, played on the same street with the same six kids. Pick one in Practice, or let the room's host pick it in Local and Online.

Agawan Base

1v1 to 3v3 · Teams · First to 3

Two teams, two bases! Tag the other team, free your own, and hold their base. The only game with the weapon deck.

Tumbang Preso

3 to 6 kids · Every kid for itself · 5 min

Knock the can over with your slipper, fetch it, and run to the other end to score. Wait too long and you become the taya, the one who guards the can.

Patintero

Teams · Runners and guards · 5 min

Get past the guards on the chalk lines, cross the middle line, reach the far end and run back. One runner home is a point for the team. Dive to slip past!

Watch your step: in every game, kids can trip while running.
